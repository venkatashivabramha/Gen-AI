QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: "I don't see this button!"
Solution: Make sure you clicked "📋 Template" in the LEFT sidebar first!

Problem: "The button says something different"
Solution: You're in the wrong mode. Click "📋 Template" on the left.

Problem: "I can't find the GUI window"
Solution: Press Alt+Tab to see all open windows, or check taskbar.


SUMMARY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

button appears on the right side!
