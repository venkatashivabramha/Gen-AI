═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOW TO SCROLL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Method 1 - Mouse Wheel (Easiest):
   1. Put mouse cursor on the RIGHT panel
   2. Roll mouse wheel DOWN
   3. Keep rolling until you see red button

Method 2 - Scroll Bar:
   1. Look for scroll bar on right edge
   2. Click and drag it DOWN
   3. Button will appear

Method 3 - Keyboard:
   1. Click once in the right panel
   2. Press "Page Down" key
   3. Or press "Down Arrow" several times
